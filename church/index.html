Your first Sunday

Walking into a new church can be awkward. Here are the questions people usually ask us.

What should I wear?
Whatever you want. You'll see jeans, shorts, and the occasional suit.
How long is the service?
About 70 minutes. We sing for 20 or so, then Pastor James or one of our other teachers speaks for about 35.
What about my kids?
We have classes from nursery through 5th grade during both services. Check in at the kids desk in the lobby and you'll get a sticker to pick them up with.
Where do I park?
The main lot off Harbor View. If you tell us you're coming, we'll hold a spot for you up front.

Let us know you're coming

Totally optional. If you fill this out, someone will meet you at the front door, help you find the kids rooms, and save you a seat. That's it. No one is going to show up at your house.

A note from Pastor James

My wife Carla and I moved to Bayview in 2011 to start Grace Harbor in our living room with nine people and a lot of folding chairs. We've grown since then, but the idea hasn't changed much. We want to be a church where it's okay to show up with questions, where people actually know each other's names, and where we take care of our neighbors.

If you've been hurt by church before, or you've never really been, I'd love to buy you a coffee after a service and hear your story.

James Whitfield, Lead Pastor

Get involved

Sunday is a good start, but most friendships here happen the rest of the week.

Small groups

About 30 groups meet in homes around town during the week. Most share a meal, talk about Sunday's message, and pray for each other. New groups start in January.

Food pantry

Every Saturday morning we hand out groceries to around 150 local families. No experience needed. Kids 8 and up can serve with a parent.

Students & kids

Harbor Kids runs during both Sunday services. Middle and high schoolers meet Wednesday nights at 6:30 for dinner, games, and a short talk.

  • Worship team Musicians and sound/video volunteers
  • Marriage prep Six-week class, offered twice a year
  • Care team Meals and visits for people going through a hard season
  • Recovery Thursday nights, 7pm, in the Fellowship Hall

Prayer requests

Our prayer team meets Tuesday mornings and prays through every request by name.
