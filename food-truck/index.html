Our Story

Started with one smoker and a dream

Marco grew up in San Antonio between his abuela's kitchen and his uncle's pit. In 2019 he bought a beat-up truck, welded a smoker to the back, and started serving brisket tacos at the Saturday farmers market. We sold out in 90 minutes.

Today we're still family-run, still smoking every cut low and slow, and still pressing every tortilla by hand.
