      About us

      Marco started Smoke & Salsa in 2019 with a used truck and a smoker his uncle welded for him back in San Antonio. Our first Saturday at the farmers market we sold out in an hour and a half and had to tape a sign to the window.

      It's still Marco on the pit most mornings, and his sister Ana runs the window. We still press every tortilla by hand, mostly because Marco won't let us stop.
